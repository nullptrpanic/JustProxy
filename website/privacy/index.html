PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Updated and effective: November 15, 2026

JustProxy is an HTTP proxy running on your iPhone. It requires no account or sign-in, includes no ads or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not upload proxy traffic, connection records, or statistics to developer-operated servers.

1. How proxy requests are handled

When you start the proxy and configure a client to use JustProxy, the app processes the client’s requests on your device and forwards them to their requested destinations. Destination services and the networks you use may process these requests under their own privacy policies. HTTPS CONNECT traffic is relayed through tunnels; JustProxy does not install certificates to decrypt HTTPS.

2. Session statistics and retention

To display session traffic, the app keeps uploaded and downloaded byte counts and the destination host, port, request method, and traffic for up to 50 recent connections in device memory. The app does not write this information to persistent files or upload it to the developer. Statistics reset for a new proxy session; records in memory are released when the app process ends.

3. Network information and permissions

The app reads currently available local network addresses so you can choose a listening address. Local Network permission is used to reach destinations on your local network. JustProxy does not request location access, read Wi-Fi network names, or request access to photos, contacts, the microphone, or the camera.

4. Your controls

You can stop the proxy from the main screen at any time. Ending the app process clears its session records from memory. There is no user account or personal profile stored on developer-operated servers to delete.

5. Website and support inquiries

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses no analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub may process technical information needed to provide its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you contact us by email, your email address and message are used to respond to your inquiry, address issues, and follow up as needed. Do not send passwords, tokens, or sensitive proxy traffic. You may request deletion of support emails you provided by contacting the address below.

6. Policy updates and contact

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. For questions about this policy or requests concerning information you provided to support, contact:

隐私政策

更新及生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日

JustProxy 是运行在用户 iPhone 上的 HTTP 代理工具。应用无需注册或登录，不集成广告或第三方统计 SDK，也不向开发者运营的服务器上传代理流量、连接记录或统计数据。

1. 代理请求如何处理

当你开启代理并将客户端配置为使用 JustProxy 时，应用会在设备上处理客户端发来的请求，并按请求转发到相应目标服务器。目标服务和你使用的网络可能按照各自的隐私政策处理这些请求。HTTPS CONNECT 流量以隧道方式转发；JustProxy 不安装用于解密 HTTPS 的证书。

2. 会话统计与保存期限

为展示会话流量，应用在本机内存中记录上传和下载字节数，以及最近连接的目标主机、端口、请求方式和流量，最多保留最近 50 条连接记录。这些信息不由应用写入持久化文件，也不上传给开发者。新代理会话会重新统计；应用进程结束后，内存中的记录随之释放。

3. 网络信息和权限

应用读取设备当前可用的本地网络地址，以提供监听地址选择。本地网络权限用于访问局域网目标。JustProxy 不申请定位权限，不读取 Wi-Fi 名称，也不申请照片、通讯录、麦克风或相机权限。

4. 你的控制方式

你可以随时在主界面关闭代理。结束应用进程可清除内存中的会话记录。应用没有开发者服务器上的用户账号或个人档案需要删除。

5. 网站与主动联系

此网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，未使用分析脚本或广告。访问网站时，GitHub 可能处理提供托管服务所需的技术信息，具体见 GitHub 隐私声明。

如果你主动通过邮件联系我们，你提供的邮箱和邮件内容将用于回复咨询、处理问题和必要的后续沟通。请勿发送密码、令牌或敏感的代理流量内容。如希望删除主动提供的支持邮件，可通过下方邮箱提出请求。
